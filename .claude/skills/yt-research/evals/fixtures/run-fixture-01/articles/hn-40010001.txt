OpenAI today announced o4-turbo, a new model in the o-series reasoning line.

On internal benchmarks, o4-turbo is 2.3x faster than o3 on HumanEval and 1.9x faster on GPQA while scoring within 1 point on both. Pricing is unchanged at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens.

"o4-turbo is designed for production workloads where latency matters," said OpenAI president Greg Brockman. "We wanted to give developers a drop-in replacement for o3 that's noticeably snappier without asking them to re-budget."

Context window remains at 200K tokens. Function calling, structured outputs, and vision are all supported. The model is available today via the API under the ID o4-turbo-2026-04-23.

Rate limits are doubled compared to o3 across all tiers. Enterprise customers on the Priority tier get a dedicated capacity pool during the first week of rollout.

No new safety fine-tunes are described in today's release. OpenAI says the safety profile is "materially identical" to o3 and points to its updated system card for details.

The company also noted that o4-turbo will be the default model powering ChatGPT's Plus tier starting next week.
