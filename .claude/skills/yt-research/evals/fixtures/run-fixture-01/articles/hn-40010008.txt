AI will change everything in 2026. I have said this before, and I will say it again, because the rate of change is now accelerating past the point at which any single person can keep up, and the implications are, I think, profound.

Every job will be affected. Every industry will be affected. Every relationship between human and machine will be affected.

I do not have specific predictions because specific predictions are a trap. The general direction is clear: more capability, more autonomy, more integration into the substrate of daily life. The details are noise.

Some will say this is hype. Some will say it is understatement. History will decide.

What I do know is that those who are paying attention will do better than those who are not. And those who are building will do better than those who are watching.

Thank you for reading my thoughts this morning.
