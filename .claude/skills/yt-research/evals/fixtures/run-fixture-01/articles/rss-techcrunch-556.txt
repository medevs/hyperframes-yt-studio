An AI coding assistant startup has raised $250 million in a Series B round at a post-money valuation of $3 billion, according to a source familiar with the deal.

The round was led by a tier-one Silicon Valley venture firm with participation from a sovereign wealth fund and several strategic corporate investors. Neither the startup nor the lead investor responded to requests for comment.

The company, founded in 2023, is focused on "enterprise-grade" AI coding assistants. It has not disclosed a customer list or product specifics publicly. Its website describes the product as "an AI pair programmer for regulated industries" with features including "SOC 2 compliance, VPC deployment, and zero data retention."

The AI coding assistant market has seen heavy funding activity over the past 12 months, with at least four companies in the space now valued at over $2 billion. Analysts say consolidation is likely as differentiation becomes harder and switching costs for developers remain low.
