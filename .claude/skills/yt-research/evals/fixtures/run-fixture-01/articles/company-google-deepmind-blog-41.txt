Today we're announcing Gemini 2.5 Pro Coder, a specialized variant of Gemini 2.5 Pro fine-tuned for software engineering tasks.

On SWE-bench Verified, Gemini 2.5 Pro Coder scores 91.2%, a 14-point improvement over the Gemini 2.5 Pro baseline of 77.1%. The model is also 38% faster on end-to-end task completion thanks to a new speculative code-execution feature that runs candidate patches in parallel.

Gemini 2.5 Pro Coder is available in preview today via the Gemini API under the model ID gemini-2-5-pro-coder. Pricing matches the base Gemini 2.5 Pro tier.

The new model is optimized for:
- Multi-file refactoring tasks that touch 10+ files
- Fault localization from test failures and stack traces
- Long-horizon coding agents operating over 30+ minutes

Key improvements over the base model:
1. Fine-tuned on 2.4M real-world PR diffs from permissively licensed repositories
2. Context window expanded to 2M tokens specifically for codebase navigation
3. New "codebase map" tool that prebuilds a symbol index before agentic tasks

We're rolling out Gemini 2.5 Pro Coder through Google AI Studio and Vertex AI today. General availability will begin in 30 days once we've completed red-team testing with launch partners.

We're also releasing the evaluation harness and the 8,400 held-out SWE-bench Verified problems used in our evaluation, so that researchers can reproduce our numbers independently.
